Learn from the beginning

AI explained in everyday language.

You do not need to know anything about technology to get started. Begin with the basics and build your understanding one step at a time.

🧠

What is AI?

Artificial intelligence is technology that can do tasks that usually need human-like abilities, such as understanding language, spotting patterns or creating content.

💬

How do I talk to AI?

You type or say what you want help with. Give it context, explain the result you want and ask follow-up questions if the answer is unclear.

✍️

What is a prompt?

A prompt is simply the question or instruction you give an AI tool. For example: “Explain photosynthesis in simple words.”

🧰

What is an AI tool?

An AI tool is an app or website that uses artificial intelligence to help with a task, such as writing, research, design or coding.

🎓

Can AI get things wrong?

Yes. AI can produce answers that sound convincing but are inaccurate. Check important information with trustworthy sources.

🔒

Is it safe to share information?

Be careful with private information. Do not enter passwords, financial details or confidential information into AI tools.

The jargon translator

AI terms without the confusion.

You may see these words when reading about AI. Here is what they mean in simple terms.

Artificial intelligence (AI)

Technology that performs tasks such as understanding language, recognising patterns or creating content.

Prompt

The question or instruction you give an AI tool.

AI model

The trained computer system that powers an AI feature — a bit like the engine inside a car.

Generative AI

AI that can create new content, such as text, pictures, audio or video.

Chatbot

A tool you communicate with by typing or speaking.

Automation

Using technology to carry out a repeated task with less manual work.

Data

Information a computer can use, such as words, numbers, images or measurements.

Hallucination

When an AI makes up information or gives an incorrect answer as though it were true.
